## Before you publish
0. **Only claim what the product really does today.** If a sentence on the site or demo promises something your product can't do yet (for example stock tracking or payment reminders that actually send), take it out or build it first.
1. Fill every `[bracket]`. Delete any answer you can't stand behind today (for example the phone answer or the "stop" terms).
2. Only say "3 spots" if you will really stop at 3. Update it as spots fill.
3. Have an attorney read the privacy page once.
