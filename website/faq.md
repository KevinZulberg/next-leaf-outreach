### Do I need training?
No. We set it up for you. We load your prices, set up your first quote with you, and show you the few screens you'll use every day. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

### How long does it take to set up?
It depends on your price list. Send us your price list (a photo is fine) and we'll tell you exactly how long it will take. [Fill in your usual time, for example "a few days".]

### Can I use my own prices and my own logo?
Yes. Your price list and your logo go in, so every quote looks like it came from your business.

### We already use Xero, Sage or Zoho. Do we still need this?
Keep them. They do your accounts. We look after the work around the money: quotes, jobs, stock and who owes you. You can export your records for your accountant at any time.

### Why not just use a free quoting tool?
Free tools are good at one thing, usually a quote or an invoice. We connect quotes, jobs, stock and payments in one place, set up for your workshop, so you're not juggling several tools. If a free tool already does everything you need, stay with it.

### How much does it cost?

### Can my team use it?
Yes. You can add your team and choose what each person can see. For example, your staff can see jobs but not your prices.

### Does it work on my phone?
[Confirm before publishing.] Yes. It works in the browser on your phone, so you can check a job or a quote from the workshop floor.

### Who owns my data?
You do. You can download your records at any time, and we don't sell your information.

### What if I want to stop?
[Confirm your terms, for example "There's no long contract. Give us notice and download your records."]

### Is it only for workshops?
It's built with workshops and trades in mind: businesses that quote, run jobs and chase payments. If that sounds like you, it will probably fit. Not sure? Ask us.

### What do I do next?
